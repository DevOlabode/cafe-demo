Our story

My mom ran a bakery in Morinville for twenty years, and her cinnamon bun recipe is still the one we use. I opened Bramble & Rye in 2017 with one oven, six tables and a lot of flour.

We're still small. Two of us come in at 5 to bake, the soup changes every day, and most people in here on a weekday morning are regulars. If it's your first time, tell us. We'll point you at whatever came out of the oven last.

DanaDana Kowalski, owner and head baker

Good to know

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. The password is on the chalkboard by the till. Over the lunch rush we ask laptop folks to give up the big tables.

Can I order cinnamon buns for a party?

Yes. Use the order form above or text us. For more than four dozen, give us 48 hours.

What can I eat if I'm gluten-free?

The soup, salad, parfait, date squares and brownies are made without gluten, and we keep gluten-free bread. We bake with wheat flour in the same kitchen, so we can't promise zero cross-contact.

Do you cater?

For small groups, yes: sandwich platters, soup, baking and coffee boxes for up to about 40 people. Text us the date and headcount.
