About us

My mom ran a bakery in Morinville for twenty years, and her cinnamon bun recipe is still the one we use. I opened Bramble & Rye in 2017 with one oven, six tables and a lot of flour.

We're still small. Two of us come in at 5 to bake, the soup changes every day, and most people who come in on a weekday morning are regulars. If you're new, say so. We'll tell you what's good today.

Dana KowalskiOwner and head baker

Common questions

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. The password is on the chalkboard by the counter. We ask laptop folks to give up big tables over the lunch rush.

Can I order cinnamon buns for a party?

Yes. Use the order form above or text us. For more than four dozen, please give us 48 hours.

What can I eat if I'm gluten-free?

The soup, salad, parfait, date squares and brownies are all made without gluten, and we keep gluten-free bread. We bake with wheat flour in the same kitchen, so we can't promise zero cross-contact.

Do you cater?

For small groups, yes: sandwich platters, soup, baking and coffee boxes for up to about 40 people. Text us with the date and headcount.
